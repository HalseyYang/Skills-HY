# Source Map

Use current official sources for regulatory advice.

## FDA

- FDA Device Approvals and Clearances: 510(k), De Novo, PMA, HDE, and related market pathways.
- FDA How to Study and Market Your Device: pathway overview and device advice.
- FDA Product Classification database: product codes and classification regulations.
- FDA 510(k), De Novo, PMA databases: predicates, summaries, special controls, decision rationale.
- FDA Breakthrough Devices Program page and guidance: BDD eligibility, interaction options, and designation process.
- FDA Q-Submission guidance: pre-submission and meeting strategy.
- FDA recognized consensus standards database.
- FDA software, cybersecurity, AI/ML, human factors, biocompatibility, sterilization, and clinical investigation guidance pages as applicable.

## EU MDR

- EUR-Lex Regulation (EU) 2017/745 consolidated text.
- European Commission Medical Devices sector pages.
- MDCG endorsed guidance documents, especially classification, clinical evaluation, equivalence, PMCF, software, UDI, and legacy-device topics.
- Harmonised standards lists for MDR.
- NANDO database for notified body scope when notified body strategy matters.
- EUDAMED/UDI guidance when registration, traceability, or vigilance topics arise.

## International And Standards

- IMDRF guidance for SaMD, clinical evaluation, cybersecurity, UDI, and adverse event terminology.
- ISO 13485, ISO 14971, IEC 62304, IEC 62366-1, IEC 60601 series, IEC 81001-5-1, ISO 10993 series, ISO 11135/11137/17665, ISO 11607, and product-specific standards as applicable.

## Source Discipline

Record source title, issuing body, publication/update date, version/status, and URL. Flag draft, superseded, withdrawn, or jurisdiction-limited sources.
