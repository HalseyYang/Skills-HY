# Strategy Checklists

## FDA Pathway

- Is the product a device under the FD&C Act?
- What is the intended use and indication?
- Is there an existing classification regulation or product code?
- Are predicates available for substantial equivalence?
- Are technological differences likely to raise new questions of safety/effectiveness?
- Is De Novo more appropriate than 510(k)?
- Is PMA expected because of Class III/high-risk profile?
- Is HDE relevant for rare disease/condition use?
- Is a Q-Submission advisable?
- Are there special controls, recognized standards, or guidance documents?

## FDA Breakthrough Devices Program

- Does the device provide for more effective treatment or diagnosis of a life-threatening or irreversibly debilitating disease or condition?
- Does it meet at least one statutory breakthrough criterion?
- Is the benefit-risk story credible with current evidence?
- What designation evidence is needed versus market authorization evidence?
- What FDA interactions would BDD unlock or accelerate?

## EU MDR

- Is the product a medical device, accessory, software device, system/procedure pack, custom-made device, or Annex XVI product?
- Which Annex VIII rule applies?
- What class follows from intended purpose and risk?
- Which conformity assessment route applies?
- Is notified body involvement required?
- Which GSPRs are most critical?
- Is clinical evaluation based on literature, equivalence, clinical investigation, PMCF, or a combination?
- What PMS/PMCF and vigilance obligations flow from the class and evidence profile?

## Software, AI, And SaMD

- Is the software driving or influencing diagnosis/treatment?
- What is the clinical decision significance and patient condition criticality?
- Is the model locked, adaptive, or continuously learning?
- What data management, validation, cybersecurity, usability, and change-control evidence is needed?
- Are PCCP, algorithm-change protocol, or model monitoring considerations relevant?

## Evidence Planning

- Bench/performance
- Electrical safety and EMC
- Biocompatibility
- Sterilization and packaging
- Usability/human factors
- Software lifecycle and cybersecurity
- Clinical evaluation/investigation
- Literature/state of the art
- Risk management
- Labeling and IFU
- PMS/PMCF/post-market commitments
