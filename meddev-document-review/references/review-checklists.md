# Review Checklists

## Universal Checks

- Intended use, indications, contraindications, user, patient population, and device description are consistent.
- Claims are supported by evidence and do not exceed the cleared/approved/certified scope.
- Standards and guidance versions are identified.
- Risk management links hazards, hazardous situations, controls, verification, residual risk, benefit-risk, and labeling.
- Verification/validation evidence maps to requirements and claims.
- Clinical evidence maps to state of the art, intended purpose, performance, safety, and benefit-risk.
- Labeling and IFU match risk controls and user needs.
- Acronyms, terminology, tables, and cross-references are consistent.

## FDA Submission Documents

- Predicate or De Novo rationale is explicit.
- Technological differences and new questions of safety/effectiveness are addressed.
- Performance testing supports substantial equivalence or reasonable assurance.
- Software, cybersecurity, biocompatibility, sterilization, EMC/electrical safety, usability, and clinical sections are included when applicable.
- Indications and labeling match the requested authorization.
- Q-Sub or deficiency responses answer each question directly and cite supporting attachments.

## EU MDR Technical Documentation

- Annex II and III coverage is clear.
- GSPR checklist has evidence references, not generic "complies" statements.
- Classification rationale cites Annex VIII rules.
- Clinical evaluation links state of the art, equivalent devices if any, clinical data, PMCF, and benefit-risk.
- PMS/PMCF plans match device class, risk, novelty, and clinical evidence maturity.
- UDI, labeling, IFU, SSCP if applicable, and post-market obligations are addressed.

## CER/Clinical Documents

- Scope matches intended purpose and claims.
- Literature search strategy is reproducible.
- Appraisal method is stated.
- Equivalent-device claims are justified under applicable rules.
- Clinical benefits, risks, side effects, and acceptability are clearly evaluated.
- PMCF rationale is specific and defensible.

## Software/AI/Cybersecurity

- Software safety class/level is justified.
- Architecture, requirements, risk controls, V&V, SOUP, anomaly list, and release/change control are coherent.
- Cybersecurity threat model, SBOM, vulnerability handling, authentication, update, logging, and security testing are addressed.
- AI/ML data, training, validation, bias, generalization, monitoring, and change-management claims are supported.
