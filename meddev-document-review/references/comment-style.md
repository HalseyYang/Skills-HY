# Comment Style

## Priority Labels

- Critical: likely submission blocker or serious regulatory/evidence risk.
- Major: likely reviewer question or significant credibility gap.
- Minor: clarity, wording, format, or editorial improvement.

## Comment Formula

Use:

```text
[Priority] Section X: <issue>.
Why it matters: <regulatory/evidence/reviewer impact>.
Suggested revision: <specific edit or replacement text>.
Evidence needed: <documents, tests, standards, literature, or rationale>.
```

## Rewrite Patterns

Weak:

```text
The device is safe and effective.
```

Better:

```text
Based on the bench performance testing, risk-control verification, and clinical evidence summarized in Sections X-Y, the available evidence supports the safety and performance of the device for the proposed intended use, subject to the limitations described in Section Z.
```

Weak:

```text
The software meets IEC 62304.
```

Better:

```text
The software lifecycle documentation has been organized against IEC 62304, including software safety classification, architecture, requirements, risk-control traceability, verification, validation, SOUP assessment, anomaly management, and release records. The specific evidence references are provided in Table X.
```

## Tone

Be direct but constructive. Do not shame the drafter. Make every comment actionable.
