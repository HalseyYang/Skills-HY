# PMS and PSUR Audit Checklists

Detailed itemized checklists for auditing PMS Plans and PSURs against EU MDR 2017/745, MDCG guidance documents, and ISO 13485.

---

## PMS Plan Checklist

### 1. Device Identification and Scope

**Regulation**: MDR Article 84(1), MDCG 2025-10 Section 4

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 1.1 | Device name and trade name | Clearly stated, matches technical documentation |
| 1.2 | Device model(s) and variants | All relevant models covered, or explicit scope boundary |
| 1.3 | UDI-DI | Listed for each device variant |
| 1.4 | Device classification (Annex VIII) | Stated class with classification rationale |
| 1.5 | Intended purpose | Matches IFU and technical documentation |
| 1.6 | Patient population | Defined where relevant (age, condition, contraindications) |
| 1.7 | Device description summary | Key characteristics, materials, technology type |
| 1.8 | Conformity assessment route | Stated for context (e.g., Annex IX + X) |

### 2. PMS System Description

**Regulation**: MDR Article 84(1), MDCG 2025-10 Section 5

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 2.1 | Organizational responsibility | Defined roles for PMS activities (who owns, who reviews) |
| 2.2 | Qualified personnel | Personnel with appropriate expertise and training |
| 2.3 | PMS system scope | What is included and excluded from the PMS system |
| 2.4 | Interface with QMS | How PMS integrates with the quality management system |
| 2.5 | Interface with clinical evaluation | How PMS data feeds into CER updates |
| 2.6 | Interface with risk management | How PMS data feeds into risk management file updates |
| 2.7 | Document control | Version control, review cycle, approval process for the PMS plan |

### 3. Data Sources and Collection Methods

**Regulation**: MDR Article 84(3), MDCG 2025-10 Section 5.2

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 3.1 | Complaints and adverse event reports | Collection method, recording system, responsible party |
| 3.2 | PMCF data | How PMCF study data is collected and reported into PMS |
| 3.3 | Literature review | Systematic or targeted literature monitoring methodology |
| 3.4 | Registry data | Use of national or international registries where applicable |
| 3.5 | Sales and distribution data | How market exposure data is obtained (units sold, geographies) |
| 3.6 | User feedback (non-complaint) | Mechanism for collecting user experience, satisfaction, ease of use |
| 3.7 | Service and maintenance records | Field return data, RMA data, servicing logs |
| 3.8 | Social media and public sources | Whether and how public reports are monitored (for Class IIb/III) |
| 3.9 | Competent authority communications | Tracking of safety communications from authorities |
| 3.10 | Data collection frequency | Defined intervals for each data source |
| 3.11 | Data quality criteria | Defined criteria for data completeness, accuracy, and relevance |

### 4. Proportionate Approach

**Regulation**: MDR Article 84(3), MDCG 2025-10 Section 5.1

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 4.1 | Class-based proportionality | Data collection effort scaled to device class and risk |
| 4.2 | Volume-based consideration | Data collection intensity reflects market volume |
| 4.3 | Risk-based consideration | Higher-risk devices have more intensive PMS activities |
| 4.4 | Justification for data sources | Each data source is justified based on risk profile |
| 4.5 | Small company / low volume justification | If reduced scope is claimed, is it justified? |

### 5. Vigilance and Serious Incident Reporting

**Regulation**: MDR Articles 87-92, MDCG 2025-10 Section 6

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 5.1 | Serious incident definition | Definition consistent with MDR Article 2(65) |
| 5.2 | Internal reporting procedure | How serious incidents are escalated internally |
| 5.3 | 2-day timeline | Procedure for serious public health threat reporting |
| 5.4 | 10-day timeline | Procedure for death or unanticipated serious deterioration |
| 5.5 | 15-day timeline | Procedure for other serious incidents |
| 5.6 | Trend reporting | Procedure for detecting and reporting trends (Article 88(4)) |
| 5.7 | Field safety corrective action (FSCA) | Defined process for initiating and executing FSCAs |
| 5.8 | FSCA effectiveness evaluation | Method for verifying FSCA effectiveness |
| 5.9 | Recipient identification | Defined process for identifying relevant competent authorities |
| 5.10 | Record keeping for vigilance | Retention periods and accessibility of vigilance records |

### 6. Signal Detection and Trend Analysis

**Regulation**: MDR Article 84(3), MDCG 2025-10 Section 5.3

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 6.1 | Signal detection methodology | Defined method (statistical, qualitative, or both) |
| 6.2 | Data aggregation | How data from different sources is combined for analysis |
| 6.3 | Statistical thresholds | Defined criteria for when a signal triggers investigation |
| 6.4 | Frequency of analysis | How often data is reviewed for signals |
| 6.5 | Escalation procedure | What happens when a signal is identified (who is notified, timelines) |
| 6.6 | Trend report triggers | Criteria for initiating a trend report to competent authorities |
| 6.7 | Documentation of signal investigation | How signal investigations are recorded and closed out |

### 7. PSUR Planning

**Regulation**: MDR Article 86, MDCG 2025-10 Section 7

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 7.1 | PSUR frequency stated | Matches the requirement for the device class |
| 7.2 | PSUR period defined | Start and end dates for each reporting period |
| 7.3 | Data scope for PSUR | Defined what data is included in each PSUR |
| 7.4 | Responsible author | Named individual or role responsible for PSUR preparation |
| 7.5 | Review and approval | Internal review process before PSUR submission |
| 7.6 | Submission procedure | How and to whom the PSUR is submitted (NB / CA) |

### 8. PMCF Integration

**Regulation**: MDR Annex XIV Part B, MDCG 2025-10 Section 5.4

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 8.1 | Reference to PMCF plan | PMS plan cross-references the current PMCF plan |
| 8.2 | Data flow from PMCF to PMS | How PMCF data enters the PMS system |
| 8.3 | Data flow from PMS to PMCF | How PMS findings influence PMCF activities |
| 8.4 | Equivalence monitoring | Whether equivalence claims are monitored through PMS data |
| 8.5 | PMCF update triggers | What PMS findings would trigger a PMCF plan update |

### 9. Risk Management Update

**Regulation**: MDR Article 84(4), MDCG 2025-10 Section 5.5

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 9.1 | Trigger criteria for risk review | Defined conditions under which the risk management file is updated |
| 9.2 | Feedback to risk management file | Process for recording PMS findings in the risk file |
| 9.3 | Benefit-risk reassessment | When and how benefit-risk is reassessed based on PMS data |
| 9.4 | FSC impact on risk file | How field safety actions feed back into risk estimates |
| 9.5 | Residual risk monitoring | Whether residual risks are specifically tracked in PMS |

### 10. FSC and Corrective Action

**Regulation**: MDR Article 85, MDCG 2025-10 Section 6.3

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 10.1 | FSC procedure | Documented process for initiating field safety corrective actions |
| 10.2 | FSC decision criteria | When an FSC is warranted vs. other corrective measures |
| 10.3 | CAPA linkage | How PMS findings feed into corrective and preventive actions |
| 10.4 | Effectiveness verification | Method for verifying that corrective actions are effective |
| 10.5 | Close-out criteria | Defined criteria for closing corrective action records |

### 11. Resource Allocation

**Regulation**: MDR Article 84(1), MDCG 2025-10 Section 5

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 11.1 | Sufficient qualified resources | Evidence that adequate personnel are allocated |
| 11.2 | Tools and systems | Defined tools for data collection, analysis, and reporting |
| 11.3 | Training requirements | Defined training for personnel involved in PMS activities |

### 12. Record Keeping

**Regulation**: MDR Article 84(5), MDCG 2025-10 Section 8

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 12.1 | Retention period | Stated retention period for PMS records |
| 12.2 | Document control | PMS plan is under document control per the QMS |
| 12.3 | Traceability | Ability to trace PMS findings to actions taken |
| 12.4 | Accessibility | PMS records are accessible to competent authorities and NB |

## PSUR Checklist

### 1. Report Header and Identification

**Regulation**: MDCG 2022-21 Section 3

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 1.1 | Device name and model(s) | Matches technical documentation |
| 1.2 | PSUR identification number | Unique reference number for the report |
| 1.3 | PSUR period | Start and end dates of the reporting period |
| 1.4 | Device classification | Stated (I, IIa, IIb, III) |
| 1.5 | UDI-DI | Device identifier per Article 27 |
| 1.6 | Manufacturer details | Name and address of the manufacturer |
| 1.7 | Notified Body (if applicable) | NB name and NB number |
| 1.8 | Report version and date | Current version, date of issue |
| 1.9 | Contact person | Name and role of the responsible contact |

### 2. Frequency Compliance

**Regulation**: MDR Article 86(2)

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 2.1 | Reporting frequency correct | Matches the class requirement (annual, biennial, as needed) |
| 2.2 | No gaps between periods | Consecutive PSURs cover the full lifecycle without gaps |
| 2.3 | Frequency justification | If deviating from standard frequency, is it justified? |

### 3. Safety Summary

**Regulation**: MDCG 2022-21 Section 4

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 3.1 | Estimated number of devices sold/distributed | Figures for the reporting period and cumulative |
| 3.2 | Number of serious incidents | Count and brief description of each |
| 3.3 | Serious incident analysis | Root cause, device involvement, patient outcome |
| 3.4 | Field safety notices issued | Number, dates, scope, and effectiveness |
| 3.5 | Recalls or withdrawals | Number, scope, and reasons |
| 3.6 | Trend reports submitted | Number, dates, and outcomes of any trend reports |
| 3.7 | Non-serious incidents summary | Aggregate count or representative cases |
| 3.8 | Near-miss or quality events | Summary of relevant quality events that did not reach patients |

### 4. Incident and Complaint Analysis

**Regulation**: MDCG 2022-21 Section 5

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 4.1 | Incident classification | Each incident classified by type and severity |
| 4.2 | Root cause analysis | Documented root cause for serious incidents |
| 4.3 | Corrective actions taken | Actions implemented in response to incidents |
| 4.4 | Open items | Outstanding investigations or pending corrective actions |
| 4.5 | Comparison with previous PSUR | Whether incident rates have increased, decreased, or stabilized |
| 4.6 | Device involvement assessment | Whether the device caused or contributed to each incident |

### 5. Literature Review

**Regulation**: MDCG 2022-21 Section 6

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 5.1 | Search methodology | Databases searched, search terms, date range |
| 5.2 | Relevant publications identified | Summary of new publications relevant to the device |
| 5.3 | Safety-related findings | Any new safety concerns identified from literature |
| 5.4 | Performance-related findings | Any new performance data that affects the benefit-risk assessment |
| 5.5 | Impact assessment | How literature findings affect the device's safety or performance profile |

### 6. Trend Analysis

**Regulation**: MDR Article 88(4), MDCG 2022-21 Section 7

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 6.1 | Incident rate calculation | Defined denominator (e.g., devices sold, exposure time) |
| 6.2 | Statistical analysis | Trend testing method (e.g., Poisson regression, control charts) |
| 6.3 | Comparison across periods | Incident rates compared to previous PSUR periods |
| 6.4 | Statistically significant increases | Any trends that triggered or should have triggered a trend report |
| 6.5 | Confounding factors | Consideration of changes in sales volume, reporting practices, etc. |

### 7. Clinical Investigation Data

**Regulation**: MDCG 2022-21 Section 8

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 7.1 | Ongoing studies listed | All active clinical investigations involving the device |
| 7.2 | Interim results | Summary of any interim data from ongoing studies |
| 7.3 | Completed studies | Summary of final results from studies completed during the period |
| 7.4 | Study deviations | Any protocol deviations, safety signals, or early terminations |

### 8. Benefit-Risk Evaluation

**Regulation**: MDCG 2022-21 Section 9

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 8.1 | Updated benefit-risk assessment | Current benefit-risk determination based on all available PMS data |
| 8.2 | Comparison to previous assessment | Whether benefit-risk has changed since the last PSUR |
| 8.3 | Residual risk acceptance | Whether residual risks remain acceptable |
| 8.4 | Risk mitigation measures | Status of previously identified risk mitigations |
| 8.5 | Identified risk factors | Any new risk factors identified during the period |

### 9. FSC Status

**Regulation**: MDCG 2022-21 Section 10

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 9.1 | New FSCs issued during period | Number, dates, scope, and reasons |
| 9.2 | Outstanding FSCs | Status of FSCs from previous periods that remain open |
| 9.3 | FSC effectiveness verification | Whether FSCs achieved their intended effect |
| 9.4 | FSC follow-up actions | Additional actions taken based on FSC monitoring |

### 10. PMCF Status

**Regulation**: MDCG 2022-21 Section 11

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 10.1 | PMCF plan reference | Current PMCF plan referenced and dated |
| 10.2 | PMCF study progress | Status of each ongoing PMCF activity |
| 10.3 | Data collected | Summary of PMCF data generated during the reporting period |
| 10.4 | Deviations from PMCF plan | Any deviations and justifications |
| 10.5 | PMCF evaluation report | Reference to the latest PMCF evaluation report (if available) |

### 11. Changes Since Last PSUR

**Regulation**: MDCG 2022-21 Section 12

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 11.1 | Design changes | Any modifications to device design, materials, or software |
| 11.2 | Labeling and IFU updates | Changes to labeling, IFU, or packaging |
| 11.3 | Manufacturing process changes | Changes to manufacturing methods, suppliers, or facilities |
| 11.4 | New indications or populations | Any expansion or modification of intended use |
| 11.5 | Regulatory changes | New approvals, certificate renewals, or scope changes |

### 12. Conclusions and Actions

**Regulation**: MDCG 2022-21 Section 13

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 12.1 | Overall safety conclusion | Statement on whether the device remains acceptably safe |
| 12.2 | Overall performance conclusion | Statement on whether the device performs as intended |
| 12.3 | Planned actions | Specific actions resulting from PSUR findings |
| 12.4 | Action owners and timelines | Who is responsible and when actions will be completed |
| 12.5 | Next PSUR date | Stated date for the next periodic safety update report |

### 13. Annexes

**Regulation**: MDCG 2022-21 Section 14

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 13.1 | Serious incident listing | Detailed table of all serious incidents |
| 13.2 | Trend analysis appendices | Statistical data, charts, and supporting calculations |
| 13.3 | Literature summary | Full list of reviewed publications with relevance assessment |
| 13.4 | FSC chronology | Detailed timeline of FSCs with scope and effectiveness data |

## ISO 13485 Cross-Reference

When reviewing PMS Plans and PSURs, the following ISO 13485 clauses should be cross-checked. The PMS system operates within the manufacturer's QMS, and the PMS documents should demonstrate alignment with these QMS obligations.

### Clause 8.2.1 — Customer Feedback

**Maps to**: MDR Article 84(3) — data collection from users and patients

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| Q1 | Feedback collection procedure | Does the PMS plan describe how customer feedback is systematically collected and recorded? |
| Q2 | Feedback evaluation | Is there a process for evaluating whether feedback relates to a safety or performance issue? |
| Q3 | Feedback documentation | Are feedback records maintained and traceable? |
| Q4 | Integration with complaints | Is feedback distinguished from formal complaints, or is it handled under the same process? |

### Clause 8.2.2 — Complaint Handling

**Maps to**: MDR Articles 87-88 — vigilance and serious incident reporting

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| Q5 | Complaint definition | Does the document define what constitutes a complaint, consistent with ISO 13485? |
| Q6 | Recording and investigation | Are complaints recorded, investigated, and documented? |
| Q7 | Cause determination | Is root cause analysis performed for complaints? |
| Q8 | Regulatory reporting triggers | Does the complaint procedure include criteria for escalating to vigilance reporting? |
| Q9 | Feedback to complainant | Is there a process for responding to the complainant? |

### Clause 8.2.3 — Reporting to Regulatory Authorities

**Maps to**: MDR Articles 87-92 — statutory vigilance obligations

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| Q10 | Awareness of obligations | Does the PMS plan acknowledge the statutory reporting requirements? |
| Q11 | Timeline alignment | Are the complaint handling timelines consistent with MDR vigilance timelines (2/10/15 days)? |
| Q12 | Trend reporting linkage | Is there a defined process for determining when a trend report is required under Article 88(4)? |

### Clause 8.2.4 — Internal Audit

**Maps to**: MDR Article 84(4) — PMS system effectiveness review

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| Q13 | PMS in audit scope | Is the PMS system included in the internal audit schedule? |
| Q14 | Audit frequency | Is the PMS system audited at defined intervals? |
| Q15 | Audit findings fed back | Are internal audit findings on PMS used to improve the PMS plan? |

### Clause 10.2 — Corrective Action

**Maps to**: MDR Article 85 — field safety corrective actions; Article 84(4) — FSC and CAPA

| # | Check Item | What to Look For |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| Q16 | Corrective action from PMS | Does the PMS plan describe how findings lead to corrective actions? |
| Q17 | CAPA linkage | Is there a clear link between PMS findings, corrective actions, and effectiveness verification? |
| Q18 | Effectiveness review | Are corrective actions verified for effectiveness, and are results documented? |
| Q19 | Close-out criteria | Are there defined criteria for closing corrective actions arising from PMS? |
| Q20 | Escalation to FSC | Does the PMS plan define when a corrective action becomes an FSC? |
