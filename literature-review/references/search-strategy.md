# Search Strategy

## Question Frames

Use one of these frames:

- PICO: population/problem, intervention/exposure, comparator, outcome.
- PCC: population, concept, context. Best for scoping reviews.
- SPIDER: sample, phenomenon of interest, design, evaluation, research type. Best for qualitative reviews.
- Topic-scope-outcome: simple fallback for technical or product research.

## Query Construction

Build queries as concept blocks:

```text
("medical device software" OR SaMD OR "software as a medical device")
AND
("clinical evaluation" OR "clinical evidence" OR validation)
AND
(FDA OR MDR OR IMDRF OR NMPA)
```

Use:

- Synonyms, acronyms, old and new terms.
- American and British spellings.
- Exact phrases for standards, guidance names, product names, and laws.
- Date filters only when the question is clearly time-bounded.
- Snowballing from references and "cited by" lists for formal reviews.

## Source Selection

Prefer:

- PubMed, Google Scholar, Semantic Scholar, Crossref, arXiv, SSRN, IEEE Xplore, ACM, Scopus, Web of Science, Cochrane, depending on domain.
- Official regulator and standards-body pages for regulatory topics.
- ClinicalTrials.gov, EU Clinical Trials Register, FDA databases, NMPA pages, and registries when relevant.

For grey literature, record why the source is credible and whether it is advocacy, commercial, draft, archived, or superseded.
